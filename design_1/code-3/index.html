Gill Sans is the most well known typeface designed by Eric Gill. It was released in 1928 and became one of the most recognizable humanist sans-serif typefaces.

Originally inspired by Edward Johnston's Underground typeface, Gill Sans combines geometric simplicity with organic details. Its balanced proportions and distinctive letterforms make it suitable for both display and editorial typography.
