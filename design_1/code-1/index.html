Typeface Name: Gill Sans

Designer: Eric Gill

Release Year: 1928

Gill Sans is the most well known of the typefaces designed by Eric Gill. It was released in 1928 and was inspired by Johnston Sans, the typeface used on the London Underground.

Its distinctive humanist style combines geometric simplicity with elegant proportions. Its versatility has made it one of the most recognizable typefaces in modern graphic design.
