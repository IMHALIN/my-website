Typeface Name: Gill Sans

Designer: Eric Gill

Release Year: 1928

Gill Sans is the most well known of the typefaces designed by Eric Gill, one of the most influential British designers of the 20th century. Designed in 1928, it was created to rival the geometric typefaces seen in public spaces, which were starting to gain popularity at the time. Built in a modernist style, the typeface features simple and clean forms, looking as if they were drawn calligraphically with natural and organic movements.

However it was designed with geometric features as well. The proportions stem from monumental Roman capitals in the uppercase and traditional old-style serif letters in lowercase.
